The simplest route is an email with two or three sentences about your situation: industry, rough size, what prompted the question. You will get a reply from a human within one working day.

In the intro call, thirty minutes by phone or video, we settle three questions. Where your company stands on AI. What would be worth examining. And whether we are the right people for it.

You will not find a contact form here, deliberately. It would mean another data processor, and a conversation does not need one.
