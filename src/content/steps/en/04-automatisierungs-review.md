At the end, a list is on the table: the workflows that can be automated in whole or in part. For each candidate we state three things. The implementation effort. The expected time saved, and with it the payback. And a recommended sequence, because not everything that is possible should come first.

We present the result to the management, and to the works council on request. Deliberately as a decision paper rather than a finished product: you decide what gets built, what waits, and what is dropped.

Whatever you commission, we implement, or we support your IT in implementing it. Whatever you do not commission stays on file as a documented candidate and is not lost by the next budget round.

That keeps control where it belongs. Our job is that you know precisely what is possible and what it costs. Not to sell you as much as possible.
