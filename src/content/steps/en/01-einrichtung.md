We start with the technical foundation: a dedicated tenant for your company, sign-in through your existing single sign-on, access rights by department. Nobody has to remember a new password, and your IT keeps control.

Along with the invitation, every employee receives a rulebook of exactly one page. It answers the question that is open in most companies: which data may be entered into an AI tool, which may not, and who decides in case of doubt. One page, because a document nobody reads is not a rule but an alibi.

We also set up a first set of scheduled tasks that save time immediately: recurring summaries, report drafts, routine input for the specialist departments. The benefit should be noticeable before the first training session, not after it. That way the project starts with a result rather than a promise.
