title: "On site"
teaser: "We sit with every department and map how the work actually gets done."
---

Process documentation describes how work is meant to happen. We are interested in how it is done. So we sit with every department, at the desk rather than in the meeting room.

We ask people to show us the real routines: which reports are produced every week, which figures are copied by hand from one system into another, which emails are written the same way again and again. The result is a map of recurring work as it actually takes place.

Every routine is assessed against two questions: how much time does it consume, and how well can it be supported or fully taken over by AI. Whatever answers both questions well is marked as a candidate for automation.

None of this works unless employees answer openly. That is why we do not appear as an audit, and why we make no commitments about headcount. People who fear for their jobs do not show you the real workflow.
