A self-rating of 2.3 out of 5 does not improve through a training day. It improves when good use stops depending on the talent of individuals.

So we translate the recurring tasks from the on-site mapping into a library of tested prompts: the monthly report, the quotation check, the answer to the same customer enquiry that arrives every week. Every prompt is tried against a real case, agreed with the people responsible for that work, and built to respect the data rules established during setup.

The library belongs to you. It sits in your tenant, is organised by department, and is maintained like a toolbox rather than an archive: new cases are added, outdated ones are removed.

That is how scattered experimentation becomes a shared standard. It is the difference between ten employees using AI somehow, and an organisation that has command of it.
